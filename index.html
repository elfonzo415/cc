Good to know

Your questions, answered

Is a civil celebrant marriage legally valid?

Yes. A marriage solemnised by an appointed Civil Celebrant of Marriages is registered under the Marriage Ordinance and has exactly the same legal standing as one held at the Marriage Registry.

How far in advance do we need to start?

The Notice of Intended Marriage must be filed at least 15 clear days before the ceremony. Four to six weeks is comfortable, and I'll tell you the exact timing for your date.

Can you marry us at our own venue?

Yes. I officiate anywhere in Hong Kong, at a hotel, club, garden, rooftop or your home, on any day and at a time that suits you.

Which languages can the ceremony be in?

English, Cantonese or Mandarin, or a blend, so every family member can follow along.

We're not in Hong Kong yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Overseas and expat couples are very welcome. I handle the notice, timing and paperwork so you arrive to a ceremony rather than a to-do list.

What does the price include?

HK$3,800 covers the ceremony, filing your Notice of Intended Marriage and the marriage certificate issued on the day. Overseas, evening and custom ceremonies are quoted on request.
